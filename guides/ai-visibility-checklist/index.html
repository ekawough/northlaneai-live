AI visibility checklist for local businesses (2026)

By Ethan, NorthLane AIUpdated 2026-11-21

Quick answer

To be visible in AI search, a local business needs five things in place: access (AI crawlers allowed, fast pages that work without JavaScript), clarity (what you do and where, stated plainly at the top of each page), consistency (the same name, address, phone and hours everywhere), proof (steady real reviews and mentions on other sites) and structure (schema markup and question-and-answer content).

Access: can AI tools read your site?

  1. Your robots.txt (the file that tells search and AI tools what they may read) does not block GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot or Google-Extended.
  2. Your main content is in the page itself, not loaded later by JavaScript.
  3. Pages load in about two seconds on a phone.
  4. You have a sitemap.xml and it's submitted to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

Clarity: do you say what you do and where?

  1. The first sentence of your home page says what you do, for whom, and where.
  2. Each main service has its own page.
  3. Each service page starts with a two- or three-sentence answer before the details.

Consistency: do the listings match?

  1. Your Google Business Profile is complete: categories, services, service area, hours, photos.
  2. Your name, address, phone and hours are identical on Google, Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing Places, BBB and Facebook.

Proof: do other people vouch for you?

  1. You ask every customer for a review, with a direct link, right after the job or sale.
  2. You reply to reviews, good and bad.
  3. You're mentioned on at least a few other trusted sites: local news, suppliers, associations, sponsorships.

Structure: can machines tell what's what?

  1. LocalBusiness (or the closest type) schema on your site, with the same details as your listings.
  2. FAQ sections with real customer questions, marked up with FAQPage schema.
  3. An llms.txt file (a plain summary written for AI tools) that summarizes your business and links your key pages. It's a small extra, not the strategy.

Then test it

Ask Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the same customer questions once a month and write down who gets named. That record is how you know any of this worked.

FAQ

What's the most important item on this list?

For most local businesses: a complete Google Business Profile and a steady stream of real reviews. They feed Google Maps, AI Overviews and many AI chat answers at once.

Can I do this myself?

Yes. Every item here can be done by an owner with time. The free check tells you which ones you're missing so you can start with those.

How often should I re-check?

Monthly. AI answers shift as the tools refresh their data, so the trend matters more than one result.
