How to get your business recommended by ChatGPT

By Ethan, NorthLane AIUpdated 2026-11-21

Quick answer

To get your business recommended by ChatGPT and other AI tools: make your website say clearly what you do and where, keep your business details identical on every listing, collect steady real reviews, add schema markup, allow AI crawlers, get mentioned on other trusted local sites, and test the same customer questions every month to see what changes.

1. Test where you stand today

Open ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google. Ask what your customers ask: “best [what you sell] in [your city],” “who does [your service] near me,” “[your service] open now.” Write down who gets named. That's your baseline.

2. Say what you do, where, in plain words

The top of every service page should say the service, the area and who you are in one or two sentences. AI tools quote clear sentences. They skip slogans.

3. One page per service and city

If you do three services in four cities, each main combo deserves its own page with real details: what's included, typical problems, rough pricing, and an FAQ.

4. Make your listings match

Your business name, address and phone should be the same on Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Facebook and your site. Mismatches make it harder for any system to be sure you're one business.

5. Get steady, real reviews

Ask every customer, right after the job, with a direct link. Reply to every review. A steady stream beats a burst.

6. Add schema and let crawlers in

Schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage) labels your info for machines. Check your robots.txt isn't blocking GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot or Google-Extended. Add an llms.txt file as a small extra.

7. Get mentioned off your own site

AI tools trust what other sites say about you. Supplier dealer locators, local news, chamber of commerce pages, community sponsorships and trade associations all count.

Then check again every month

Ask the same questions in the same tools. AI answers shift, so the trend matters more than any single day.

FAQ

How long does it take for ChatGPT to start recommending a business?

There's no fixed timeline. Some changes, like listing and profile fixes, can show up within weeks as the tools refresh their data. Others take months. Track it monthly.

Can I pay to be recommended by ChatGPT?

Not as a normal organic recommendation. The way in is being clearly described, well reviewed and widely mentioned.

Do I need to be on Yelp?

It helps. AI tools and Apple Maps pull from Yelp and other directories, so a complete, accurate listing is worth having even if you don't pay for ads there.
