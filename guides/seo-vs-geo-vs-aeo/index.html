SEO vs GEO vs AEO: what business owners need to know

By Ethan, NorthLane AIUpdated 2026-11-21

Quick answer

SEO gets your pages ranked in Google's regular results and the Map pack (the map with three local businesses at the top of Google). GEO (generative engine optimization) gets your business named inside AI-written answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your answer pulled straight into Google's AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice assistants. For a local business, the same base work drives all three: a complete Google Business Profile, one clear page per service and city, consistent listings, real reviews and schema markup.

The short version

A customer who needs what you sell has three ways to find you now. They Google it and tap one of the three businesses on the map. They ask ChatGPT “who's the best one near me.” Or Google shows them an AI Overview at the top of the page before any links. Each of those has a name in marketing. You don't need the names. You need to show up in all three.

SEO: ranking in Google

Search engine optimization is the old game, and it still matters most for local service work. For a local business the biggest levers are your Google Business Profile, a page for each service and city, reviews, and a site that loads fast on a phone.

GEO: getting named by AI chat tools

Generative engine optimization is about being the business an AI tool names when someone asks for a recommendation. These tools pull from websites, review sites, directories and Google data. They lean toward businesses that are described clearly and the same way in many places.

  • Say exactly what you do and where, in plain words, near the top of each page.
  • Keep your name, address and phone identical on Google, Yelp, BBB, Angi and your site.
  • Get mentioned outside your own site: local news, supplier pages, community groups.
  • Don't block AI crawlers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot in robots.txt, the file that tells them what they may read.

AEO: being the answer

Answer engine optimization is about writing content in a shape that can be lifted out and quoted. Put a direct, two- or three-sentence answer under each question heading, then explain. Add FAQ sections with real customer questions. Mark them up with schema so machines know what they are.

What to do first

  1. Fill out every field of your Google Business Profile, including services and service area.
  2. Make one page per main service, with a short answer at the top.
  3. Fix any listing where your name, address or phone doesn't match.
  4. Ask every happy customer for a review, every time.
  5. Ask ChatGPT and Google the questions your customers ask, write down who gets named, and check again next month.

FAQ

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. AI tools still lean heavily on what ranks and what's listed on Google and review sites. Good SEO is the base GEO is built on.

Does llms.txt help?

It's a cheap, harmless file that gives AI tools a clean summary of your site. There's no proof yet that it changes rankings, so treat it as a small extra, not the strategy.

Which matters most for a local business?

Your Google Business Profile and reviews. They feed the Map pack, Google's AI Overviews and many AI chat answers at the same time.
