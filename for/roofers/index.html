Quick answer

For a roofing company, showing up in Google and AI answers comes down to a complete Google Business Profile with every service listed and recent job photos, a separate page for each job type (leak repair, roof replacement, tile, shingle, flat roofs, inspections) and each main city you serve, matching listings on Yelp, BBB, Angi and Nextdoor, steady real reviews, and schema markup, the hidden labels that tell Google and AI tools what's on each page. Because the job is expensive, reviews and clear proof of licensing and insurance carry extra weight.

Where the jobs leak

A competitor shows up in the map results at the top of Google for “roof repair near me” and you don't.
You asked ChatGPT for the best roofer in your area and it named someone else.
Your best jobs are on your phone's camera roll, not on your Google profile or your site.
Estimate requests sit for a day, and the homeowner signs with whoever answered first.

What we'd do for you

  • Test 20 real homeowner questions in Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and record who gets named
  • Fix your Google Business Profile: categories, every service, service area, recent job photos
  • Build a page for each roof type and job type, and each main city you serve
  • Make your name, address and phone match on Google, Yelp, BBB, Angi and Nextdoor
  • Add the hidden labels that tell Google and AI tools your hours, services and location, and let them read your site
  • Set up fast replies to estimate requests and automatic review requests in GoHighLevel, the texting and customer software we use
