Quick answer

For a plumber, showing up in Google and AI answers comes down to a complete Google Business Profile with every service listed and accurate hours, a separate page for each job type (drain cleaning, water heaters, leak repair, repipes, emergency calls) and each main city you serve, matching listings on Yelp, BBB, Angi and Nextdoor, steady real reviews, and schema markup, the hidden labels that tell Google and AI tools what's on each page. Emergency callers don't leave voicemail, so a missed call needs a text back in seconds.

Where the jobs leak

Another plumber shows up in the map results at the top of Google for “plumber near me” and you don't.
Your Google profile says you close at 5, so you're left out of “open now” answers at night.
Your site has one “Services” page instead of a page for drains, water heaters, leaks and repipes.
You're under a sink when the phone rings, and that caller books the next plumber.

What we'd do for you

  • Test 20 real homeowner questions in Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and record who gets named
  • Fix your Google Business Profile: categories, every service, real hours including emergency hours, photos
  • Build a page for each job type and each main city you serve
  • Make your name, address and phone match on Google, Yelp, BBB, Angi and Nextdoor
  • Add the hidden labels that tell Google and AI tools your hours, services and location, and let them read your site
  • Set up an automatic text back when you miss a call, plus automatic review requests, in GoHighLevel, the texting and customer software we use
