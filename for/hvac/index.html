Quick answer

For an HVAC company, showing up in Google and AI answers comes down to a complete Google Business Profile with every service listed, a separate page for each service (AC repair, furnace install, heat pumps, maintenance plans) and each main city you serve, matching listings on Yelp, BBB and Angi, steady real reviews, and schema markup, the hidden labels that tell Google and AI tools what's on each page. Then a missed call needs a text back in seconds, or the homeowner calls the next company.

Where the jobs leak

A competitor shows up in the map results at the top of Google for “AC repair near me” and you don't.
You asked ChatGPT for the best HVAC company in your area and it named someone else.
Your site has one “Services” page instead of a page for AC repair, furnaces, heat pumps and maintenance.
You're in an attic when the phone rings, and that caller books someone else.

What we'd do for you

  • Test 20 real homeowner questions in Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and record who gets named
  • Fix your Google Business Profile: categories, every service, service area, photos
  • Build a page for each service and each main city you serve
  • Make your name, address and phone match on Google, Yelp, BBB, Angi and Nextdoor
  • Add the hidden labels that tell Google and AI tools your hours, services and location, and let them read your site
  • Set up an automatic text back when you miss a call, plus automatic review requests, in GoHighLevel, the texting and customer software we use
