Quick answer

For an auto repair shop, showing up in Google and AI answers comes down to a complete Google Business Profile with every service listed, a separate page for each service (brakes, diagnostics, oil changes, AC, transmissions, smog checks) and the makes you work on, matching listings on Yelp, Apple Maps, Carfax and RepairPal, steady real reviews with replies, and schema markup, the hidden labels that tell Google and AI tools what's on each page. Drivers search by problem and by make, so both need to be written on your site in plain words.

Where the jobs leak

Another shop shows up for “mechanic near me” and yours doesn't.
You specialize in certain makes, but nothing online says so.
Your site lists “auto repair” instead of a page for brakes, diagnostics, AC and smog.
The phone rings while everyone's under a car, and that driver calls the next shop.

What we'd do for you

  • Test 20 real driver questions in Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and record who gets named
  • Fix your Google Business Profile: categories, every service, hours, photos of the shop and team
  • Build a page for each service and for the makes you work on
  • Make your name, address and phone match on Google, Yelp, Apple Maps, Carfax and RepairPal
  • Add the hidden labels that tell Google and AI tools your hours, services and location, and let them read your site
  • Set up an automatic text back when you miss a call, plus automatic review requests, in GoHighLevel, the texting and customer software we use
