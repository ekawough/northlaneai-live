What is GEO?

GEO stands for generative engine optimization: getting your business named inside AI-written answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude.

What is AEO?

AEO stands for answer engine optimization: shaping your content so Google's AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice assistants can lift your answer and show it directly.
