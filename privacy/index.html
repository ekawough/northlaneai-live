Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-21

The short version: we collect what you type into the form so we can do your free check and reply to you. We don't sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.

Who this is

NorthLane AI is a marketing studio in Southern California, run by Ethan. Questions about this page: [Your email].

What we collect

  • What you send in the free-check form: your name, business name, website, the area you serve, what you want to sell more of, your email, and your phone number if you add it.
  • How you got to the form: which page and button you clicked, the first page you visited, and the site that sent you here, if any. This tells us which pages are useful. It is sent only when you submit the form.
  • Basic server records: the company that hosts this site keeps standard technical logs, like IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and block abuse.

What we don't do

  • No advertising cookies and no tracking across other websites.
  • No selling, renting or trading your information.
  • No adding you to a mailing list because you asked for a free check.

This site does not run an analytics tool right now. If that changes, it will be a cookie-free one and this page will say so. The site remembers the first page you visited in your browser's session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab.

How we use it

  • To run your free AI visibility check and send you the results.
  • To reply to you and, if you ask, quote work.
  • To see which pages lead to form submissions.

Who else sees it

Only the tools we use to run the business: the website host, the form and email delivery service, and our customer system (GoHighLevel). They process the information for us and are not allowed to use it for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

If we work together, for as long as you're a client plus what tax and business records require. If we don't, we delete form submissions within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Your choices

Email [Your email] to see what we have about you, fix it, or have it deleted. If you live in California, state law gives you these rights, and we honor the same request from anyone.

Children

This site is for business owners. It isn't meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
