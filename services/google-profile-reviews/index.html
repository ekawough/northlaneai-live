Quick answer

Google Business Profile and review management means keeping your Google listing complete, accurate and active, and asking every customer for a review in a way that follows Google's rules. For a local business it is where we start, because the same profile and reviews feed Google Maps, Google's AI answers and AI chat tools like ChatGPT.

FAQ

Is it OK to ask customers for reviews?

Yes. Google allows it and encourages it. What's not allowed is paying for reviews, writing fake ones, or only asking customers you know are happy.

Can you remove a bad review?

Only Google can, and only if it breaks their rules, like spam or a review for the wrong business. We can flag those. For a fair bad review, the best move is a calm, short reply.

How many reviews do I need?

There's no magic number, but in BrightLocal's 2026 research about half of consumers said they wouldn't use a business with fewer than 20. Steady new reviews matter more than one big burst.

I don't have a Google Business Profile yet. Can you set one up?

Yes. We'll create it, get it verified with you, and fill in everything.
