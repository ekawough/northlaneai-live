FAQ

Why not WordPress or Wix?

They work, but they're heavier, need plugins and updates, and often load slow on phones. A static site is faster, safer and costs almost nothing to host. If you already have WordPress, we can work with it.

Can I edit it myself?

Yes. We can set it up so you can change text and photos, or you can send us changes on the care plan.

Do I have to take the care plan?

No. You can take the finished site and run it yourself. The care plan is for owners who would rather text a change than log in anywhere.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain, the files and the accounts are in your name.
