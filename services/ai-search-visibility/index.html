Quick answer

AI search visibility means your business shows up in regular Google results and gets named in AI answers. SEO gets you ranked. GEO (generative engine optimization) gets you mentioned by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your answer pulled into Google's AI Overviews and voice assistants. For a local business, all three come down to the same work: clear service pages, a complete Google Business Profile, consistent listings, real reviews and structured data.

A competitor shows up in the map results at the top of Google and you don't.
You asked ChatGPT for the best business like yours in your area and it named someone else.
Your website has one “Services” page instead of a page for each job you do.
Your business name, phone or address is different on Yelp, Google and your site.

FAQ

How long until I see results?

Google Business Profile and listing fixes can show up in weeks. Ranking new pages usually takes a few months. AI answers change as the models refresh, so we track them monthly instead of promising a date.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No, and nobody honest can. What we can do is fix the things AI tools use to decide who to name: clear pages, consistent listings, reviews and structured data. Then we show you the before and after.

What's the difference between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO is ranking in regular Google results. GEO is getting named inside AI-written answers like ChatGPT and Perplexity. AEO is getting your answer pulled directly into Google's AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice assistants. For a local business, the same base work helps all three.

Do I need a new website?

Not always. If your site is slow or has one page for everything, a rebuild is usually faster than patching it. We'll tell you which after the free check.
